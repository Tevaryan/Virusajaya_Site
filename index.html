What we raise

Two herds, one careful farm system.

ViRusaJaya focuses on goat feedlots for near-term livestock supply and deer herd development for long-term premium farm value. The customer story is simple: healthy animals, reliable care, and a transparent view of the herd.

Goat Feedlots

Young goats are sourced, fed and grown through a structured fattening cycle with focus on condition, weight gain and readiness for seasonal demand.

Animal care

How we look after the animals.

🌿

Structured feeding

Napier grass, palm kernel cake and pellet planning are used to secure feed supply, manage costs and support steady livestock growth.

💧

Water and shade

Farm infrastructure is planned around practical basics: water availability, sheltered spaces and comfortable housing areas.

🧼

Clean, easy-to-maintain housing

Future goat feedlot design includes plastic slatted flooring, overhead sprinklers, UV lights and raised non-wood structures to improve cleaning and hygiene.

👀

Daily observation

Animals are monitored for condition, movement, appetite and behaviour so that weak or stressed animals can be identified earlier.

🤲

Low-stress handling

The customer experience should reflect the farm’s care approach: calm handling, clean viewing, and honest explanation of each animal’s condition.
